# Product: The Master Barber (BarberWeb)

## Purpose
A premium digital gateway for a high-end, traditional barbershop. It facilitates elite grooming bookings and showcases the heritage and precision of the shop's master barbers.

## Users
- **The Modern Gentleman:** Values craft, time, and a high-end grooming experience. Expects a seamless, premium retreat.
- **Master Barbers:** Skilled artisans requiring a sophisticated platform to manage their elite clientele.

## Brand Voice
- **Warm yet Mechanical:** Professional and precise like a straight razor, but welcoming like a luxury sanctuary.
- **Opinionated:** Clearly stands for heritage and excellence over generic modern trends.
- **Sophisticated:** Cinematic, premium, and authoritative.

## Strategic Principles
1. **Precision First:** Every pixel and interaction must feel intentional and sharp (0px roundedness).
2. **Heritage through Modernity:** Balance classic serif typography (Noto Serif) with clean, functional sans-serif (Manrope).
3. **Cinematic Atmosphere:** Use "Dark Mode by Default" with high-contrast desaturated imagery to create depth and luxury.
4. **Editorial Pacing:** Avoid generic grids; prioritize asymmetrical layouts and generous "breathing room" (Section Gaps).

## Register
brand

## Aesthetic Lane
High-end Editorial / Luxury Boutique. Avoid "SaaS-cream" or generic "AI slop" templates.
